Gautam, Buddha, and the New Conquerors of Bengal
Best friends Gautam and Buddha are the cross-dressing stars of the New Conquerors, a bawdy traveling theater in rural Bengal. Sex between men is illegal in India, but life on the road affords Gautam and Buddha plenty of adventures. At home, though, Gautam's steely wife and Buddha's jealous boyfriend aim to tie their men down to more conventional roles. Visually arresting and unexpectedly funny, Gautam, Buddha, and the New Conquerors of Bengal challenges our assumptions about poverty, India, and gender roles.
